# Humans give feedback after a first deliverable

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Source: `meetings/2026-11-20-kickoff.md`

## Decision

Humans wait for a first deliverable before they give feedback.

## Why

One person said that corrections during a task often break the work of the agent. Another person wanted to correct the agent as soon as it goes in the wrong direction. The team did not agree.

## What it means for agents

No rule yet. A human decides when to give feedback.
