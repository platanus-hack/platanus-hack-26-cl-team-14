# The mock is a guide, not the goal

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Source: `meetings/2026-11-20-kickoff.md`

## Decision

The mock shows the look and the story. The product uses real data where real data exists.

## Why

A real simulation needs real data. The mock can show things that we cannot simulate with real data.

## What it means for agents

If the mock and the real data do not agree, tell the team in Discord. Propose the nearest real option. Do not use fake data without telling the team.
