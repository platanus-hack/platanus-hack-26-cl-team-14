# Show street activity when the camera is close

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-22
- Source: [Bruno's direction](https://discord.com/channels/1573514576400752640/1573915515787935856/1573940312114462833)

## Decision

Make cars and walkers legible at street and nearby district distances. Keep
the city view clear of individual figures.

## Evidence

Use the saved street and Metro demand at the selected city time. Station
walkers follow mapped access routes and represent modeled entries. Compare
Rotonda Grecia and Tobalaba with Larraín at the same camera and time before
and after each change.
