# Pitch case: swap the MUT and the Estadio Monumental

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Source: Mac (Francisco) in the Corte 4 thread, 16:15 UTC: https://discord.com/channels/1573514576400752640/1573688250797658172/1573733563491291157

## Decision

Stop the animated mock. The pitch shows the real simulation.
The main case, and the focus of all the simulation work, is: put the Estadio Monumental (Colo-Colo) where the MUT is, and the MUT where the Monumental is.
Show who arrives at each place, how, from where, and what social dynamics follow, at both places.
A smaller second case: a navigable Mapocho river with public transport, and how people would move along it during the year.
A third case comes later from Mac.

## Why

A human direction. It replaces the 15:54 UTC order to iterate only on the mock.
One strong case shown in detail is better than many shallow cases.

## What it means for agents

- Work on the real engine, data and native view, not on the HTML mock.
- Before a task, check that the swap case (or the Mapocho case) needs it.
- The Mapocho case starts with research (`context/research/`), then the smallest engine change it needs.
