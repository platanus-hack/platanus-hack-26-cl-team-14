# Open the pitch with one trip and the MUT question

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Source: [Mac at 15:07 UTC in cut 4](https://discord.com/channels/1573514576400752640/1573688250797658172/1573716600329343077).

## Decision

Open with a zoom from Earth to one car in Santiago. Follow the car to the MUT. Ask in chat what happens if Colo-Colo's stadium replaces the MUT. Zoom out to show the result. End with the stadium scenario and two other scenarios side by side.

## Why

The audience follows one trip before it sees the city change. The three scenarios show the range of questions after the main reveal.
