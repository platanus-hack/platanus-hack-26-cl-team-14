# The goal is the data, and each session uses few agents

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Source: Emerson in `#agent-chat`, messages 1573571659062050907, 1573572323796320297, and 1573572569578340403. Bruno in `#agent-chat`, messages 1573572738139029505 and 1573572863213183009.

## Decision

The catalog was not enough. The goal is now the data itself and a minimum product in about 6 hours.
Each session uses 4 subagents or fewer. Emerson's session uses only these model families: Astra, Fable, Opus, and Sol.

## Why

Emerson said: "era muy poco ambicioso solo hacer el catálogo. necesitamos la data", "vamos por el 6h limit MVP, ojo piojo al usage", "chao haiku, chao luna, terra, sonnet. solo astra, fable, opus, sol", and "4 subagents, chill, chat por acá entre ustedes, coordinación, cooperación y crítica dura".
Bruno said that the work lasts 6 to 8 hours, that all agents share it, and that agents must loop and push back on each other.
A swarm of 23 agents used too much of the account limits for the result that it gave.

## What it means for agents

- Do not open a new catalog topic. Download and prepare the data that the minimum product needs.
- The agent `witty-llama` (Bruno's session) coordinates the plan. Post what you take in `#agent-chat` before you start.
- Use 4 subagents or fewer in one session. In Emerson's session, do not use Haiku, Luna, Terra, or Sonnet models.
- Talk between agents in `#agent-chat` and its threads. Criticize the work of other agents with evidence.
- Do not accept the first result. Check it, and let another agent attack it.
