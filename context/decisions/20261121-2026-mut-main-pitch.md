# Make the MUT swap the main pitch case

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Source: [Mac’s pitch direction in Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1573514576400752640/1573815827617288294/1573842156240502958)

## Decision

Give the MUT and Monumental swap the main demo slot. Show the navigable Mapocho and the full Metro closure side by side on one slide. Use them to show that the same simulation engine can answer different city questions.

## Why

The MUT swap gives the audience time to see the intervention and its effect on one trip. The other two examples show the range of the engine in one view.

## What it means for agents

- Use matched views to show the site today, the buildings cleared for the stadium, and where the stadium would sit. Show transit arrivals and the final walk before the chart.
- Use one saved experiment in each tile of the two-case slide. Give each tile a short title and a result with its unit and time window.
- Verify the physical move and arrivals from the saved replay. The current replay accepts the modeled jobs and match location. The stadium move still needs V1 support for a footprint that crosses block boundaries. [Engine evidence](https://discord.com/channels/1573514576400752640/1573809606277337280/1573840662925676659)

## Proposed rehearsal order

| Pitch time | What the audience sees |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:12 | Santiago and the question of how a change affects the people who travel through it. |
| 0:12–0:35 | Match day at the existing Monumental. Show where the MUT is and hold the Tobalaba frame so the audience can recognize it after the edit. |
| 0:35–1:10 | Show the footprint in that same frame. Clear the buildings it covers, place the stadium, and state that the model also swaps the jobs of the two blocks. |
| 1:10–1:40 | Show the same crowd at both sites. Follow one arrival from public transport to the venue. Label individual paths as illustrative if the model does not provide them. |
| 1:40–1:57 | Show the estimated fan-mode comparison. Read one result from the rebuilt cached pair. |
| 1:57–2:32 | Mapocho and Metro share one slide. Show a ferry at the pier and the Metro service change. State that both cases use the same engine. |
| 2:32–2:50 | Show the evaluation slide with available evidence. |
| 2:50–2:55 | Close the story. |
| 2:55–3:00 | Reserve five seconds for transitions. |

The rehearsal schedule is a proposal. The Metro historical card placement and full narrated timing require a check.

## Proposed spoken lines

- **Opening.** «El partido empieza mucho antes del pitazo. Empieza cuando miles de personas salen de sus casas».
- **MUT.** «Este es el Monumental, en Macul. Y este es el MUT, al lado del Metro Tobalaba. ¿Qué tendría que cambiar para jugar el mismo partido acá?». Hold the site. «Para hacerle espacio al estadio, despejamos su huella. También intercambiamos los empleos de las dos manzanas en el modelo». Follow one arrival. «Fíjense en el último tramo, desde el transporte público hasta la entrada». Read one verified change from the saved results.
- **Two examples.** «Usamos el mismo motor para hacer otras preguntas. ¿Qué pasa si el Mapocho se vuelve una ruta de transporte? ¿Y si cierra todo el Metro durante la mañana?». Point to the change in each panel.
- **Evaluation.** «Para saber cuánto se acerca el modelo a Santiago, vamos a comparar sus predicciones con datos observados. Los puntajes están pendientes».
- **Close.** «Antes de cambiar Santiago, podemos ver a quién le cambia el viaje».
