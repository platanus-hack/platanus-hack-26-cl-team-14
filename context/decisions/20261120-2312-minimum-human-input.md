# Agents work with minimum human input

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Source: `meetings/2026-11-20-kickoff.md` and a team member on 2026-11-20

## Decision

Agents do all the work. Humans give input only on taste (what looks good, what goes in the pitch) and on direction (if the product and the pitch go the correct way).

## Why

The team gives all the construction to agents. Agents create and manage their own tasks, so that humans do not become the bottleneck. Humans are better at judging what looks good. Agents are better at implementation.

## What it means for agents

Decide technical questions yourself and write the reason. Ask humans only for taste or direction, in a release or at a checkpoint.
