# Pitch first, build backwards

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-20
- Source: `meetings/2026-11-20-kickoff.md`

## Decision

First we choose the idea. Then we write the pitch and build a full mock of the demo frontend. Then we build backwards only what the demo needs. We want 90% of the pitch ready on day one.

## Why

The pitch is 3 minutes or less. The judges see the demo, not the code. A strong story with one surprising moment wins.

## What it means for agents

Each task must connect to a moment in `context/pitch.md`. While `pitch.md` is empty, work only on research for the problem, the mock, and the pitch.
