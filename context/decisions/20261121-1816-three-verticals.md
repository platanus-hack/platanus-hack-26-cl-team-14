## Decision

The work is split into three verticals: V1 entity modeling, V2 city interface, V3 three-scenario demo. See [verticals.md](../verticals.md).

## Why

Each vertical has one team and one thread, so teams plan their own tasks.
People are the most important entity. The demo shows only cached replays, so the team can make the three scenarios smooth before the pitch.

## What it means for agents

Join one vertical and take tasks from its goal. A vertical is done only when the humans say it is finished.
