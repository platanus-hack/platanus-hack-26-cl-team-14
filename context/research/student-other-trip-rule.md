# Proposed weekday study and errand trips

Register this rule in the data thread before any fit. Brave-falcon owns the engine change. Start from the exact accepted general-engine baseline at integration. Record the commit, inputs, and full-precision baseline error. Score one candidate. The earlier L5 candidate added school enrollment to the afternoon worker profile. Its held-out error rose by 75.46 boardings. [L5 record](station-l5-rejected.md)

## Who travels and where

- **School.** Reuse the 1,277,336 existing RM-to-RM school enrollment records. Keep each home and destination comuna from `school_od.csv`. This margin includes preschool. Within the destination comuna, use all seats in `schools.csv` to assign the school. At that school, the preschool share is `(enrollment - enrollment_no_preschool) / enrollment`. Use separate preschool and school travel profiles. A school with zero seats receives nobody.
- **Higher education.** Use the Census 2024 block count `n_asistencia_superior` for home weights. It exists in the same archive used by `tools/pack/zones.py`. Use the geolocated SIES in_person count as campus weight. Exclude hybrid and remote counts from daily placement because the number of campus days is unknown. Report enrollment without a point separately. Use the smaller of the eligible resident and campus margins as the maximum number of study roles. This is a synthetic RM-internal approximation. The sources do not measure the overlap. Allocate the margin to homes and campuses in proportion to their respective weights. Assign points to EOD sectors using the survey zone polygons. Pair the two margins by iterative proportional fitting. Seed the matrix with weighted EOD higher-education home-sector × study-sector trips plus one trip distributed across all pairs in proportion to the two margins. This gives unsupported pairs positive weight. Report the final mass they receive. Within a sector use the published weights. The SIES sede comuna alone does not establish where a student lives.
- **People with two roles.** Derive work-and-study shares by home comuna from Census person aggregates, restricting work to the same RM commuter definition as the baseline. Assign study roles to the corresponding share of existing worker IDs before allocating the remaining study roles to resident slots. Keep the work and study roles distinct on that ID. Apply the same overlap rule to school learners who work. Count a resident once in demographic outputs.
- **Shopping, health, and public errands.** Give trips to Census residents, including workers, students, and residents with neither role. Use EOD normal-weekday rates per resident, including people who made zero trips. Match EOD destination sector by home sector and purpose. Within the chosen sector, sample eligible mapped points uniformly. For shopping pool shops, supermarkets, and malls. For health pool hospitals and clinics. For public errands use public offices. Keep mixed units such as beds and floor area out of the weights. Record unsupported sector or kind mass explicitly. Use stable synthetic assignments between baseline and scenario.

## Time, mode, and trip chains

Use the EOD dictionaries and the two expansion factors in `tools/pack/work_departures.py`. For trips multiply the trip and person normal-weekday factors. For resident denominators use the person factor alone. Use education institution codes 1–3 for preschool, 4 for school, and 6–8 for higher education. Use trip purposes 3 for study, 5 for health, 11 for shopping, and 12 with destination activity 7 for public errands.

Build aggregate profiles from complete home → activity → home pairs in the same diary, with consecutive trips, a return purpose of 7, and matching household and initial origin zones. Exclude incomplete, overlapping, and unsupported pairs and report coverage. Use the joint observed outbound and return modes and half-hours. Round each local `HoraIni` and `HoraFin` down to its half-hour. Retain the order across midnight. Assign pairs to eligible resident IDs without overlap with any assigned work or other tour. Carry forward unmet pair mass as unplaced. An enrollment can produce zero trips on a weekday.

Use `ModoAgregado` for city travel. For the first station candidate, count only Metro-only legs (code 4) with distinct reachable entry and exit parents. Both ends use the existing inverse-distance walk weights within 1,000 m, with a 50 m floor. Use products of entry and exit weights, omit same-parent pairs, and normalize the remaining weights per eligible leg. The trip start and end half-hours approximate Metro entry and exit times. This first candidate omits mixed-mode Metro boardings from its explicit contribution. Output only aggregate groups.

## Fit on 95 stations and score once

1. Select station IDs with `holdout == false` in `metro_training.json`. Filter April purpose rows to these IDs **before** grouping or reading their counts. The April 15 targets measure estimated-purpose trips at their final transit stop. The frozen station scorer measures average-weekday boardings at entry.
2. Construct two daily training arrival targets, ESTUDIO and OTROS. Multiply each station's purpose count by a pooled EOD coverage share. For that share, the numerator counts eligible, outward Metro-only study or covered errand legs. The denominator counts all trips in that broad purpose whose last motorized stage is Metro. Map EOD 3–4 to ESTUDIO and 5, 6, 8–14 to OTROS. Report this crosswalk as an assumption. Estimate each share from regional survey expansion weights before looking at April targets. Keep HOGAR out of calibration. Use diary returns for its contribution.
3. For each broad purpose separately, fit one nonnegative multiplier to the 95 daily station arrival counts by unweighted least squares through zero. The predictor is outward modeled Metro exits. Expand missing station/purpose rows to zero for all 95 stations. If its squared sum is zero, that purpose contributes zero. Apply the same multiplier to outward and return **station contributions**. Keep person counts, trip rates and modes at their survey values. Report station-arrival error by half-hour as a diagnostic. Do not search across tolerances, spatial radii, or model variants.
4. The existing station baseline already represents all purposes. Let B be that prediction and C be the calibrated candidate entries by origin station and departure half-hour. For half-hour t, sum B and C over the same 95 parents. If both totals are positive, let k = min(1, sum(B)/sum(C)) and f = k × sum(C)/sum(B). Predict (1−f) × B + k × C at every parent. If either total is zero, retain B for that half-hour. Freeze k and f on the baseline for scenario comparisons. This replaces an estimated share of all-purpose demand and preserves the training total per bin. It cannot fix a network-wide error in the time profile.
5. Run the frozen scorer on the 31 held-out parents and 1,123 observed cells. Accept only if absolute error falls by at least 1,000 boardings against the recorded baseline. Report the signed change even if it is worse. One Wednesday of inferred purpose, 2012 survey behavior, OSM coverage, and the limited tours remain sources of uncertainty.

Inputs inspected for this proposal include the EOD trip, person, household, stage and parameter headers, the INE block and person headers, and the source definitions in the [data pack](../../data/mvp/README.md), [station model](../../engine/README.md) and [April arrival pack](../../data/validation/trip_purpose_2026_04/README.md). In 25,000 local April source records the end code equaled twice the local hour plus the half-hour offset. No arrival or boarding fit was run for this proposal.
