# Floor area and nonagricultural properties

`data/mvp/land_use.csv` contains floor area by commune in 2016, a detailed breakdown for La Florida in 2017, Santiago municipal blocks in 2021, and regional property counts by commune in 2026. Select a year and area type before summing or joining. Each source covers a different geography or date. A blank means the source does not report that measure. Zero is a published value.

| Area type | Year | Key | Source and coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `comuna` | 2016 | Five-digit INE CUT | All 52 RM communes. Seven source groups, plus the published total. |
| `comuna` | 2017 | 13110 | La Florida. SII construction areas by individual use. |
| `municipal_block` | 2021 | `santiago2021-` + municipal layer `OBJECTID_1` | 1,458 municipal blocks in CUT 13101. Each row gives floor area in m². The source reports 48 blocks with zero total. |
| `comuna` | 2026 | Five-digit INE CUT | SII second-semester count of all nonagricultural property tax rolls, including exempt properties. Sanity check against the published RM total gives 3,702,224. |

The municipal road capacity study gives SII floor areas for all 52 communes in table 3-2. It reports housing, commerce, and education and culture separately. The table groups industry with mining (`industry_and_mining_m2`), and public administration with offices and health (`public_admin_office_health_m2`). It groups sport, hotels, worship, transport and miscellaneous uses in `other_group_m2`. Its `excluded_group_m2` includes warehouses, parking, common areas, agricultural classes and unidentified uses. Individual offices, health facilities and industry cannot be recovered from these combined totals. The published total includes every group. The 52 printed commune totals sum to 256,617,213 m². The report prints 256,617,216 m² for the region. Rounding differences within a commune reach 3 m². The CSV preserves the printed figures. [Road capacity study, chapter 3, pp. 3-2 and 3-8](https://appx.laflorida.cl/transparencia/documentacion/Inf_Final_ECVLF_cap3.pdf)

Table 3-1 reports the full set of La Florida's 2017 individual uses from the 2018 SII cadastre. The published total is 10,684,696 m². The six named uses and `other_m2` sum to 10,684,516 m². `agriculture_m2` holds the remaining 180 m². Other uses include 416,541 m² of unknown use. No version of this table reports the number of tax properties. Table 3-3 also reports combined uses in 51 local transport zones. Their identifiers differ from the EOD 2012 geography. No verified crosswalk to the 2024 census blocks was available. [Road capacity study, tables 3-1 and 3-3](https://appx.laflorida.cl/transparencia/documentacion/Inf_Final_ECVLF_cap3.pdf)

The Municipalidad de Santiago publishes the complete use breakdown. Its web scene labels the unscaled fields as floor area and the fields ending in `_2` as densities per hectare. Housing uses `H`, offices `O`, commerce `C`, industry `I`, education and culture `E`, and health `S`. Other uses include storage, parking and unclassified space. The sum of the seven columns equals the published `TOTAL_M2` in all 1,458 blocks. The total is 29,721,080 m², including 3,818,230 m² of offices. The municipality supplies the floor-area layer. Its item record gives no cadastre semester or method for allocating mixed-use properties. The municipal polygons have their own boundaries and IDs. [Municipal layer](https://services9.arcgis.com/zqDAjElD9y8GPDk4/arcgis/rest/services/TOTAL_DESTINOS_M2_CONSTRUIDOS_2021_gdb/FeatureServer/0) · [Municipal map labels](https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/5a6d746ddef14a30a119af1466a7543a/data?f=pjson)

The SII publishes three property-count tables at the same URL. The builder reads the nonagricultural table and matches commune names to the Census, including SII's “Til Til” and INE's “Tiltil”. The file header supplies the year. These counts measure tax rolls. A building can contain many rolls. [SII table](https://www.sii.cl/sobre_el_sii/estadisticas/csv/ebbrrc_16.csv)

The SII transparency portal sends the detailed commune download to its tax login before users can select a commune. The public SII dictionary distinguishes parcel land area from construction-line surface, which can use m² or m³. The original SII roll would be needed to verify a regional breakdown of offices and health and counts for individual blocks. [SII access page](https://www.sii.cl/destacados/transparencia/index.html) · [SII file dictionary](https://www.sii.cl/bbrr/descargas/estructura_detalle_catastral.pdf)

The OCUC 2018 layer has area columns by use for 34 urban comunas. We excluded it from the floor-area data because the publisher gives no units or calculation method for those columns. After removing repeated keys, 73,604,638 of 530,305,431 units fall under vacant land. In Santiago alone, OCUC reports 1,071,353 for vacant land. The municipal 2021 file gives zero built m² for that use. [OCUC layer](https://services9.arcgis.com/kKJR3Qt68ohAWuet/arcgis/rest/services/DESTINOS_SII_2018/FeatureServer/0)

To refresh the CSV, install Poppler for `pdftotext` and run `python3 -I tools/pack/land_use.py` from the repository root. SII replaces its public CSV each semester.
