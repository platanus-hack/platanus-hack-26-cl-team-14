# A non-football audience at Parque O'Higgins

Lollapalooza at Parque O'Higgins ran from 29 through 31 March 2019. Fundación Imagen de Chile reports 250,000 people for the festival. Use that figure as a measure of the full festival's scale. The source gives no single-day count, unique-person count, or comuna-of-origin table. The 60,000-person engine prompt is a separate scenario assumption. [Festival dates and official transport plan](https://www.interior.gob.cl/noticias-regionales/2019/03/29/autoridades-fiscalizan-lollapalooza-e-inspeccionan-inedito-escenario-solar/) · [Imagen de Chile annual report](https://www.marcachile.cl/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/MEMORIA-2019-IMAGEN-CHILE-30.04-baja_compressed-2.pdf)

| Model input or target | Public evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Attendance | Festival-wide total above. Use a dated gate count to score one day or an arrival window. |
| Venue access and service | The official plan recommends Metro Rondizzoni and Toesca. It adds weekend bus frequency on services 506, 507 and 510 from 11:00 to 20:00 and extends weekend Metro to midnight. These describe planned supply. |
| Audience catchment | The plan names comunas served by the bus reinforcement. It gives no weights for where festival attendees live. |
| Modes and observed arrivals | The published DTPM 2019 catalog lists April and August samples and no sample for 29–31 March. I found no festival mode-share or station-entry table for these dates. [DTPM trip matrices](https://www.dtpm.cl/index.php/documentos/matrices-de-viaje) |

Use a supplied audience profile when its source and date match the event. Until one is available, keep origins and mode shares unknown or label any synthetic profile as an assumption. A general leisure participation rate can check how often people attend an event across many venues. It cannot identify this festival's audience.
