# Santiago housing, prices, land, and public space

Research date: 2026-11-21. Purpose: find real inputs for urban social dynamics before the pitch is fixed.
Scope changed in the Discord goal thread: another agent covers building geometry, culture, and activity places.

Santiago commune, Greater Santiago, and the Metropolitan Region are different areas. For national spatial data, select the agreed urban boundary. Do not use the whole region as the city.

## Sources

“Opened” means the source page or service responded. It does not mean a complete dataset was downloaded or inspected.

INPUT sets an initial condition or a mechanism. TARGET is an observed quantity for comparison with model output. These roles are proposed.
No source has a verified ingestion workflow. Visible web observations are identified below; metadata alone is not a data sample.

## Use and remaining gaps

First verify actual green-area and settlement records before selecting them as spatial inputs. Use Census housing totals from the people catalog to constrain housing counts.
Use IPV for observed price trends. Keep SII assessed values, VMV estimates, and listing asking prices in separate variables.

No open address-level sale-price or rental-history file was confirmed. The nearest public option with visible observations is the BCCh aggregate price series; its ingestion remains unverified.
The zoning download needs repair or replacement. Portal access alone does not establish permission for bulk reuse.
No ingestion code, paid access, bulk downloads, or tests were added. This is a source catalog, not an exhaustive inventory.
