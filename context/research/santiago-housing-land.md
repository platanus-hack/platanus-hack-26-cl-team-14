# Santiago housing, prices, land, and public space

Research date: 2026-11-21. Purpose: find real inputs for urban social dynamics before the pitch is fixed.
Scope changed in the Discord goal thread: another agent covers building geometry, culture, and activity places.

Santiago commune, Greater Santiago, and the Metropolitan Region are different areas. For national spatial data, select the agreed urban boundary. Do not use the whole region as the city.

## Sources

“Opened” means the source page or service responded. It does not mean a complete dataset was downloaded or inspected.

INPUT sets an initial condition or a mechanism. TARGET is an observed quantity for comparison with model output. These roles are proposed.
The [independent sample audit](sources/verify-housing-land.md) read records for IPV, permits, green areas, settlements and supplemental SII transfers. The [housing-price report](sources/housing-listings.md) records the full SII download. These samples establish access and fields, not a complete ingestion workflow.

## Use and remaining gaps

Green-area, permit and settlement samples now establish spatial access. Full extraction, pagination and record definitions still need work. Use Census housing totals from the people catalog to constrain housing counts.
Use IPV for observed price trends. Keep SII assessed values, VMV estimates, and listing asking prices in separate variables.

Public rol-level sale-transfer history exists in SII F2890. It still needs location, property-use and multi-rol deed treatment before residential price analysis. No rental transaction history was confirmed. Asking prices and short-stay nightly prices are separate measures; the price report records their limits.
The zoning download needs repair or replacement. Portal access alone does not establish permission for bulk reuse.
This reconciliation adds no downloads or tests; it cites the published peer samples. It is a source catalog, not a complete ingestion pipeline.
