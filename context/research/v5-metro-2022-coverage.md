# 2022 data coverage for a second Metro interruption

The morning Metro closure scene needs a second event to check how the model moves station entries when part of a line closes. The November 2024 Line 1 result remains fitted evidence. The 2022 cases below give no additional score.

## Line 3, 7 April 2022

Metro reported normal service at 07:00 after about 40 minutes without service between Los Libertadores and Hospitales. Red reinforced surface service. The report gives an approximate start. It suggests that 06:30–07:00 fits the interruption. A dated onset notice is still missing. [Metro restoration notice](https://twitter.com/metrodesantiago/status/1512023213747978246) · [24 Horas report](https://www.24horas.cl/nacional/metro-normaliza-servicio-en-linea-3-tras-falla-tecnica-en-estacion-conchali-5253638)

The [frozen panel](https://discord.com/channels/1573514576400752640/1573863187672207411/1573881703448187052) uses Los Libertadores, Cardenal Caro, Vivaceta, Conchalí, Plaza Chacabuco and Hospitales. Use 6 April as the reference and 5 April to check variation between weekdays. Before scoring, confirm the open terminal and represent the 2022 line before its northern extension. The emergency bus reinforcement remains a limit on the comparison.

DTPM lists 4–10 April. Its stages ZIP returns HTTP 404. The journeys ZIP is available. A byte-range check found one Deflate64 CSV member of 1,643,120,443 compressed bytes. A 4 MiB prefix decoded 43,134 complete 4 April rows. The dictionary and the CSV header give a service, boarding stop and boarding time for each of the first four stages. The journey expansion factor is present. A first Metro leg count would use that journey factor. It still needs a check against dated station entries before use as a score. No 5–7 April counts were extracted. ## Lines 1 and 5, 11 August 2022

DTPM lists 8–14 August and the stages ZIP responds. Metro's quoted updates report a power cut and then an operating network with frequency recovering. The posts are at 07:16 and 07:29 local time. They establish no full half-hour with a defined set of closed stations. The 16 August Line 2 derailment falls outside the data week.
