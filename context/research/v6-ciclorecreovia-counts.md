# CicloRecreoVía participation evidence

The V6 replay needs entries by access point and time. The public sources below cover the city's overall event scale and a sample of participant behavior.

| Source | What it measures | Use in V6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Organizer's Santiago page](https://ciclorecreovia.cl/santiago/) | Reports 30,000 people per Santiago weekend and a Sunday schedule from 09:00 to 14:00. Gives no count method, observation date, or split by route, direction, or time. | Citywide context and the program's usual hours. A dated closure notice must set the hours for a specific replay. |
| [2018 Santiago study](https://www.scielo.cl/article_plus.php?lng=es&pid=S0034-98872018000400451&tlng=es) | Interviewed 401 users at rest stations on five routes in August and September 2016. In that sample, 68.4% said they usually cycled and the mean reported activity time was 113 minutes. | Historical sensitivity inputs. The interview counts cannot give the number of people who entered a route. Activity time does not measure time at a queue or shop. |

## First replay

Use the dated route and closure schedule. Declare the route's entry profile, participant mix, and stop or visit rules as assumptions until observations cover them. Keep 30,000 attached to the entire Santiago program.

For a route-level check, request the event date, count location, interval, direction and mode. Ask whether each value counts entries, unique people, or passings. People can pass a counter twice on an out-and-back route. Use passings to check segment flow and entry counts to check the total that entered. Shop visits and sales need their own observations.
