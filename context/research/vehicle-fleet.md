# Private car permits by issuing comuna

The INE annual results list 2024 as the latest workbook. Table 4, column E, counts permits under “Automóvil, Station Wagon y Todo Terreno” in the private transport category. INE includes hearses and jeep-type vehicles in this column. It counts pickups, vans, motorcycles and taxis in separate columns. INE marks the 2024 figures provisional. [INE annual series](https://www.ine.gob.cl/estadisticas-por-tema/comercio-y-servicios/permisos-de-circulacion) · [2024 workbook](https://www.ine.gob.cl/docs/default-source/parque-de-vehiculos/cuadros-estadisticos/resultados/2024.xlsx?sfvrsn=5801b9b3_4)

To reproduce `data/mvp/vehicles.csv`, select the 52 rows marked “Comuna” in the Región Metropolitana de Santiago section of table 4. Match column C to the names in `data/mvp/communes.csv` after removing accents and normalizing case. Keep the Census CUT. Divide column E by the Census 2024 household count and round to six decimal places. The 52 car counts sum to the published regional subtotal of 1,672,081. The 52 household counts sum to 2,599,621.

INE assigns each permit to the municipality that issued it. Census households live in the comuna. Alhué issued 52,079 car permits for 2,770 resident households (18.801083 per household). The ratio measures permit issuance per resident household. A household car-ownership probability requires a source that records where the household lives.
