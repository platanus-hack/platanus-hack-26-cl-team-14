National sources cover the Metropolitan Region (RM). Filter them to the chosen simulation boundary.
The Santiago commune, Greater Santiago, and RM are different areas. RM survey estimates do not describe Greater Santiago alone.

## Source catalog

Roles are proposals for source selection. INPUT supplies a model condition or parameter.
TARGET supplies an observed aggregate or survey estimate for comparison, subject to its sampling limits.
No final score is selected. Data used to fit a model are not independent validation of that model.
Each entry now has bounded row evidence from the verification report. Sample-only checks and unresolved geographic allocations remain explicit.
These checks establish access and fields, not a complete ingestion pipeline or valid model calibration.

## Use in the simulation

Start with Census block totals and commune-area microdata. Use CASEN and ESI to estimate traits that the census does not measure.
Any allocation of survey traits to blocks is a model assumption. It is not a measured block-level income distribution.

Use SII branch addresses as candidate work destinations. Use employment and commuting data to constrain their weights.
Do not put all workers at headquarters. MINEDUC enrolment can weight school destinations; it does not measure daily attendance.
Transform MINSAL points and polygons to the same coordinate system before spatial joins.

## Method notes and remaining gaps

- [CASEN 2024 usage note](https://observatorio.ministeriodesarrollosocial.gob.cl/storage/docs/casen/2024/Nota_uso_bases_de_datos_Casen_2024.pdf): national, regional, and urban/rural representation; commune-level precision limits remain even with auxiliary geography.
- [ENE codebook, July 2026](https://www.ine.gob.cl/docs/default-source/ocupacion-y-desocupacion/bbdd/libro-de-codigos/codigos-ene-2020.pdf): documents commune identifiers and warns that survey design guarantees national and regional representation.
- A complete public worksite-level count of workers was not found. SII addresses plus commuting constraints are the nearest identified option.
- Attendance schedules, healthcare capacity, and visits still need separate datasets; school enrolment is present in MINEDUC. This catalog does not infer them from facility counts.
- Source pages establish availability, not permission for every redistribution. Preserve provider attribution and inspect each dataset's terms before shipping data.

This is a bounded source catalog. It does not claim every relevant source or a working ingestion pipeline.
