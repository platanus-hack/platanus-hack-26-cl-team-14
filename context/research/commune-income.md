# Household income by residence commune

`data/mvp/income.csv` covers the 52 Metropolitan Region CUTs. CASEN 2015 treated 26 as survey domains. Their 14,997 sampled households support a historical estimate of mean monthly household monetary income, in November 2015 Chilean pesos. The other 26 rows have empty estimates and status `outside_survey_domain`. The sampling design targeted poverty, so precision varies for income. For example, Talagante has 201 sampled households and a 95% interval from CLP 392,372 to 1,257,570. A mean describes a commune and cannot establish an individual household's income. [INE sampling design, 27 December 2016, Annex 4](https://observatorio.ministeriodesarrollosocial.gob.cl/storage/docs/casen/2015/Metodologia_de_Diseno_Muestral_Casen_2015.pdf)

## Source and calculation

These are our estimates from the ministry's public survey files. The 95% intervals and standard errors are our calculations. [CASEN 2015](https://observatorio.ministeriodesarrollosocial.gob.cl/encuesta-casen-2015) · [Main survey](https://observatorio.ministeriodesarrollosocial.gob.cl/storage/docs/casen/2015/casen_2015.dta.zip) · [Commune file](https://observatorio.ministeriodesarrollosocial.gob.cl/storage/docs/casen/2015/casen_2015_provincia_comuna.dta.zip)

1. Select household heads (`pco1 == 1`). Join their `folio` and `o` to the commune file. The join has one row per sampled household. Match `comuna` to CUT. All 52 names match the census after case and accent normalization.
2. For `region == 13` and `car == 1` (self-represented commune), take the mean of `ymonecorh` using `expc` as the weight. This is autonomous household income plus monetary subsidies. It excludes imputed rent. A value of zero counts in the mean.
3. Calculate the sampling variance by Taylor linearization. For each household in the commune, calculate its weight times its deviation from the mean, divided by the commune's total weight. Sum by survey cluster (`varunit`) within survey stratum (`varstrat`). Clusters outside the commune contribute zero. In each stratum, multiply the sample variance of cluster totals by the number of clusters. Sum across strata. Every stratum contributing to these estimates had at least two sampled clusters.
4. The standard error is the square root of this variance. The interval uses the two-sided 95% Student t critical value with degrees of freedom equal to the commune's sampled cluster count minus its sampled stratum count. Round each result to the nearest peso.

An independent study published means for the same 26 communes in 2021. Table 2 agrees within one peso for 24. It assigns the other two values to the opposite names. The official survey and census both identify Pedro Aguirre Cerda as 13121 (CLP 706,540) and Peñalolén as 13122 (CLP 1,138,247). [Published estimates, Table 2](https://www.scielo.cl/article_plus.php?lng=es&pid=S0718-83582021000200054&tlng=es)

## Which years support the measure?

The ministry published an older commune table in CASEN 2006, workbook 4. The 2017, 2020, 2022 and 2024 income workbook indexes list regional tables and contain no commune income table. The 2024 survey note warns that direct commune estimates can be unreliable or biased. Its published small-area estimates measure poverty rates. SINIM's CASEN indicator group lists only the poverty rate (ISOC001). The newest survey wave with a published table of commune household means that we verified was 2015.
