# Line 1 cut on 30 November 2024

This case could check the Metro scene with a second event after the development comparison of 18 November. It has a documented service change and a complete half-hour. The public DTPM sources checked here do not give the event counts.

## Service and data

- At 10:54 on Saturday 30 November, Metro reported that Line 1 served Pajaritos–Los Dominicos only. San Pablo and Neptuno lost Line 1 service. The San Pablo transfer to Line 5 was suspended. Red ran a support loop from San Pablo to Pajaritos. Metro reported full Line 1 service at 11:36. The 11:00–11:30 bin falls fully inside the announced cut. [Operator notices reported by La Tercera](https://www.latercera.com/nacional/noticia/metro-de-santiago-suspende-operaciones-en-tramo-de-la-linea-1-por-falla-tecnica-de-tren/KT7OL6JUDNB6FDKUV6OJ3V2QPA/)
- The DTPM catalog lists 18–24 November. The ZIP central directory confirms daily stage files for those seven dates. Saturday 23 November is present. Saturday 30 November is absent. The directory is in the last 65,536 bytes. [Catalog](https://www.dtpm.cl/index.php/documentos/matrices-de-viaje) · [Stage archive](https://www.dtpm.cl/descargas/modelos_y_matrices/Tabla-de-Etapas-%20Nov24.zip)
- The November boarding workbook groups counts by day type, mode, stop, comuna, and half-hour. The sheet has no individual date field. Its notes call the weekend dates “sábado 25 y domingo 26,” which do not match the 2024 calendar. The workbook cannot identify a count for the event day or establish a matched Saturday. [Boarding workbook](https://www.dtpm.cl/descargas/modelos_y_matrices/Subida_Paradero_Estacion_2024.11.xlsb)

## What would make this scoreable

Obtain Metro entries by line, station, and half-hour for 30 November and a comparable Saturday. Pajaritos is the open endpoint. Neptuno is a service-availability check. San Pablo needs line-specific counts because station totals can include Line 5. A dated observation for the bus loop would help interpret reallocations. Keep this case out of the score until the dated entry counts are available.

A separate candidate fits a published day. On 21 April 2025 Metro announced that trains would skip Universidad de Santiago after 19:00. Confirm when it reopened and choose the comparison period before counting entries.
