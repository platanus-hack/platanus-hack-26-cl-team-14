# Observed bus speeds in Santiago

CityRed estimates commercial speeds from bus GPS observations. Each cell covers one service variant, a section of its route (500 m by default) and a 30-minute interval. CityRed divides the total distance observed in the cell by the total travel time. The interface filters by date, service, operator and type of day. A cell without observations has no speed. Bus commercial speed includes stops. Applying it to cars would require independent evidence. [Method](https://documentacion.city-red.cl/aptto-dtpm-velocidades/metodolog%C3%ADa/) · [User guide](https://documentacion.city-red.cl/aptto-dtpm-velocidades/guia_uso/)

The public CityRed site redirects to a sign-in page. The public guide shows the map and matrix views. We found no open file with April 2026 values, distances and segment IDs. The engine has no measured segment times from this source. [CityRed](https://city-red.cl/)
