# Santiago Metro closure, October 2019

## Observed service

Metro announced the full closure at 19:20 local time on Friday, 18 October. The network had 136 stations. All Metro stations stayed closed on Saturday 19 and Sunday 20. On Monday 21, service resumed at 20 Line 1 stations between Pajaritos and Los Dominicos, skipping Los Héroes. Use 19–20 October for the full closure and 21 October for a separate partial-service comparison. [DTPM 2019 management report, pp. 108–109 and 113](https://www.dtpm.cl/descargas/memoria/InformeGestion_2019.pdf) · [Ministry service plan for 21 October](https://www.subtrans.gob.cl/plan-de-transporte-publico-de-santiago-durante-jornada-del-21-de-octubre/)

## Public counts

- The DTPM trip and stage catalog lists 8–14 April and 5–12 August for 2019. Its October sample is from 2018. April and August give pre-event reference days, including weekends. The catalog has no event-day matrix. [DTPM trip and stage catalog](https://www.dtpm.cl/index.php/plan-operacional-historico2/24-programas-de-operacion/228-decimo-tercer-programa-de-operacion-etapa-de-regimen)
- DTPM table 33 gives mean daily bus departures from 16 October to 31 December against 18 August to 16 September 2019. Weekdays averaged 52,429 versus 71,171 (−26.3%). Saturday averaged 40,044 versus 49,466 (−19.0%). Sunday averaged 33,695 versus 43,068 (−21.8%). These are vehicle departures averaged over each period by day type. [DTPM 2019 management report, p. 112, table 33](https://www.dtpm.cl/descargas/memoria/InformeGestion_2019.pdf)
- DTPM table 34 gives 228,960,548 recorded bip! validations for bus, Metro, and train combined from 16 October through 31 December. The report projects 353,819,182 for normal service over the same period from 2019 averages (−35.3%). Validations count fare transactions. The table groups the full crisis period. [DTPM 2019 management report, p. 112, table 34](https://www.dtpm.cl/descargas/memoria/InformeGestion_2019.pdf)

## Replay comparison

Use the weekend closure as a check on service availability. The closure replay should show zero active Metro stations and zero Metro boardings during the full closure. These are expected values from the documented service state. Compare the baseline and closure with the same date type, time window, and demand assumptions. If the baseline uses a weekday, label the pair as a weekday scenario informed by the 2019 closure.

Report changes in bus, car, walking, and unserved trips as model estimates. The curfew, bus service changes, and road disruptions also changed travel during the crisis. To check redistribution against observations, obtain event-day counts with the same dates, mode, area, and time bins as the replay and a comparable open-service baseline.
