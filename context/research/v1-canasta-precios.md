# Basic basket and supermarket price levels

Bruno's request: a reference basic-basket price and a price level for each supermarket brand in the OSM data, with low-cost chains compared to mainstream chains. Research date: 21 November 2026.

## Canasta Básica de Alimentos (CBA)

- **October 2026: $93.678 per person per month.** Change: +0,9 % in the month, +5,2 % in 12 months, +5,0 % since December 2025. Source: Ministerio de Desarrollo Social y Familia, "Valor de la Canasta Básica de Alimentos y Líneas de Pobreza, informe mensual, octubre de 2026", Cuadro 1. https://observatorio.ministeriodesarrollosocial.gob.cl/storage/docs/cba/nueva_serie/2026/Informe_CBA_2026_10.pdf (read 21 Nov 2026; press report of 17 Nov 2026: https://radiopaulina.cl/2026/11/17/canasta-basica-de-alimentos-subio-52-en-un-ano-y-llego-a-93-678-por-persona-en-octubre)
- The value is national. The ministry does not publish a Santiago value.
- Poverty lines in the same report (per equivalent person): $257.076 for non-renters, $379.838 for renters. Family of 4: $678.427 non-renters, $1.002.398 renters.
- Method (metodología 2024): spending on food by the first income quintile in the IX EPF (INE, 2021–2022), less 50 % of ultra-processed food, less items that fewer than 10 % of households buy, scaled to 2.000 kcal per person per day. The ministry updates it every month with the INE CPI food division. It is a cost of food, not a price at one chain.
- Earlier 2026 values: January $89.570, March $90.261. https://www.theclinic.cl/2026/04/15/canasta-basica-de-alimentos-sube-a-90-261-en-marzo-y-presiona-las-lineas-de-pobreza-en-chile/

## OSM supermarkets → chain → index

Input: `data/mvp/activity_points.csv`, `kind = supermarket` (OSM `shop=supermarket`), 508 rows. The file has no `brand` column. Only `name` holds the chain. Match the name case-insensitively and without accents, in this order (first match wins):

| Order | Name pattern (regex on the normalized name) | Chain | Index | Rows |
| ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | `acuenta` | aCuenta | 76 | 18 |
| 2 | `lider express\|express de lider` | Lider Express | 88 | 78 |
| 3 | `^lider$` | Lider (hiper) | 100 | 44 |
| 4 | `mayorista 10` | Mayorista 10 | 76 | 20 |
| 5 | `\bsuper ?10\b` | Super 10 | 76 | 9 |
| 6 | `central mayorista` | Central Mayorista | 76 | 2 |
| 7 | `\balvi\b` | Alvi | 76 | 19 |
| 8 | `\bekono\b` | Ekono → Lider Express | 88 | 27 |
| 9 | `santa isabel` | Santa Isabel | 78 | 70 |
| 10 | `\bunimarc\b` | Unimarc | 110 | 51 |
| 11 | `\btottus\b` | Tottus (includes Tottus Express) | 105 | 28 |
| 12 | `\bjumbo\b` | Jumbo | 112 | 21 |
| 13 | `don salva` | Don Salva (Cencosud hard discount) | 76 | 3 |
| 14 | `^mass$` | Mass (hard discount) | 76 | 4 |
| 15 | `ahorra` | Ahorra Food Depot | 76 | 6 |
| 16 | `\berbi\b` | Erbi (regional chain) | 100 | 7 |
| — | anything else, including 7 blank names | Unknown | 100 | 101 |

Notes:

- `mayorista 10` comes before `super ?10`, so "Supermercado Mayorista 10" does not become Super 10. Plain "Mayorista" and "KMayorista" stay unknown.
- "Supercongelados. Sta Isabel" stays unknown. It is probably a frozen-food shop on calle Santa Isabel, not the chain.
- **Default for unknown brands: 100.** No source gives a price level for independent supermarkets, minimarkets or "mall chino" stores. The SERNAC 2021 RM study (supermarkets about 11 % above ferias and mercados) compares outlet types, not minimarkets, so we do not use it. https://www.sernac.cl/portal/604/w3-article-63379.html (15 Sep 2021)
- Matched rows: 407 of 508 (80 %).

## Limits

- **Mixed baskets.** The rows come from three different baskets (barbecue products in 2023 and 2026, an unknown 30 items in 2026). The index is a rank with rough gaps, not a measured price ratio.
- **The low-cost tier (76) is unverified.** Its only source is a blog with no method. No official source separates aCuenta, Alvi, Mayorista 10 and Super 10. Alvi sells with quantity price steps, so a household price depends on volume (SMU Memoria 2020: https://assets.ctfassets.net/63tonbtz0lhl/3x9OJv2bWorIDPEwAOajmC/1fd38c2fd22d418cc4094372098e95cf/Memoria_Integrada_SMU_2020.pdf).
- **Santa Isabel (78) is probably low for a full basket.** It comes from one meat-heavy basket in 2023. An undated cart on payright.cl also puts Santa Isabel first, but it is not a study. https://payright.cl/
- **Lider Express (88) below Hiper Lider is from one source.** The SERNAC page gives the same value to Lider Express and Tottus ($43.491). That can be a copy error.
- **Tottus and Jumbo disagree between sources.** BiQu 2026 puts both above Lider. SERNAC 2023 and the blog put Tottus below Lider.
- **Stale OSM names.** Walmart's October 2026 list has no Ekono stores, so the 27 Ekono points are probably now Lider Express (or closed). Mayorista 10 points are being converted to Super 10 or Alvi. OSM "Lider" can also tag some Express stores.
- **Central Mayorista** sells only to businesses. Households do not shop there. Exclude it from household destinations if the model needs that.
- **National, not Santiago.** The CBA and SERNAC 2023 are national values. Only BiQu (online) and SERNAC 2021 (RM) are Santiago-relevant.
- **Not found:** an official 2024–2026 study with basket totals for all chains, and any ODEPA price data by chain.
