# MUT visitor evidence

The MUT ↔ Monumental scenario needs visitor arrivals by time, origin and mode. The sources below cover volume and nearby transport. A site-level origin and mode table is still missing.

| Source | What it reports | Use in the swap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MUT Sustainability Report 2025, pp. 4, 11 and 48](https://mut.cl/cms/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MUT-SUSTAINABILITY-REPORT-2025.pdf) | The operator reports nearly 800,000 monthly visitors on average in 2025. The key figures page reports 248,737 visitors in the last week of May 2026 and 19,143 weekday passengers per day through the Tobalaba access, as of 29 March 2026. | Use reported volume as context for the existing MUT. The different periods and populations prevent a Metro share calculation. Access traffic can include people passing through. The report gives no count of unique visitors. |
| [MUT Sustainability Report 2025, p. 48](https://mut.cl/cms/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MUT-SUSTAINABILITY-REPORT-2025.pdf#page=48) | The March 2026 customer study names Las Condes, Vitacura and Ñuñoa as areas with market potential. | Ask the operator for any aggregate visitor tables from this study. The public summary gives no observed shares by origin or mode. |
| [Entel Digital Crowds case study](https://www.enteldigital.cl/blog/analisis-predictivo-y-la-geointeligencia-detras-del-exito-del-mut) | Publishes profiles of people moving around Tobalaba and walking catchments. | Use as context for the surrounding area. The public case study gives no visitor origin and mode table for MUT entrances. |
| [DTPM Trip Matrices](https://www.dtpm.cl/index.php/documentos/matrices-de-viaje) | The April 2026 sample covers 11–17 April. Trip and stage files estimate alightings from fare records and GPS. | Use trips ending near Tobalaba and the Monumental as separate public transport checks. A station or stop identifies a network location. It cannot identify the passenger's venue or trip purpose. |
| [SECTRA Santiago Origin–Destination Survey](https://www.sectra.gob.cl/info-territorial/region-metropolitana-de-santiago/) and [repo shopping model notes](student-other-trip-rule.md) | The 2012 survey includes purpose, mode and zones. The repo uses shopping trips by home and destination sector. | Use sector shopping trips as a historical reference for a mall scenario. The survey predates the MUT and cannot validate visitors at either swap site. |

Keep the demo's work trips and event returns tied to their own model outputs. Before adding visitor flows, request aggregate entrance counts by hour, weekday and weekend, plus survey counts by origin comuna, arrival mode and purpose. Ask how the survey distinguishes visitors, workers and people passing through. Keep the operator's published counts as context until those groups can be matched.
