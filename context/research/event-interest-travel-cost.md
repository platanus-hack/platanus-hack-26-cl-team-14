# Model interest, travel cost and optional attendance

Scope: explain who might go to a new venue in Santiago. Compare the same synthetic person at two locations. Preserve the fixed-attendance stadium comparison. [Bruno's request](https://discord.com/channels/1573514576400752640/1573809708047798364/1573863736979230792) · [Research thread](https://discord.com/channels/1573514576400752640/1573863864624349259)

## Published methods

- ActivitySim includes zero optional outings in its random-utility frequency model. Its destination model then uses the accessibility of travel modes for an outing. Santiago's ESTRAUS also uses a combined measure of the available modes when estimating flows. Neither reference supplies a coefficient for interest in a given concert. [ActivitySim frequency](https://activitysim.github.io/activitysim/v1.5.1/dev-guide/components/non_mandatory_tour_frequency.html) · [ActivitySim destination](https://activitysim.github.io/activitysim/v1.5.1/dev-guide/components/non_mandatory_destination.html) · [ESTRAUS and project models](destination-choice.md)
- FHWA's special-event handbook uses residents within a travel-time market area as a planning estimate. It prefers observed ticket-holder origins or a survey of similar event patrons for an event-specific split. That survey distinguishes home from work and other trip origins. [FHWA market area](https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/fhwaop04010/chapter3_05.htm) · [FHWA patron origins](https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/publications/fhwaop04010/chapter5_03.htm)

## Santiago evidence

- ENPCCL 2024 covers urban residents aged 15 and older. The live music questions record visits in the past 12 months (`MUSICA_VECES`), whether the latest visit was in the home region (`MUSICA_REGION`), where the trip began (`MUSICA_TRASLADO`), and a reported duration band (`MUSICA_TIEMPO`). The release includes microdata and weights. Publish RM cell counts and weighted shares before using a subgroup. The latest attended event gives a travel-time check among attendees. It does not identify the travel time of people who stayed home or a given venue's commuter origins. [Release](https://observatorio.cultura.gob.cl/index.php/2025/10/20/encuesta-nacional-de-participacion-cultural-y-comportamiento-lector-enpccl-2024/) · [Questionnaire, pp. 43–44](https://observatorio.cultura.gob.cl/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Cuestionario-CAPI-ENPCCL-2024.pdf#page=43)
- ENPCCL also asks about wanting to attend and missing a cultural activity. Of the people who reported unmet desire for some cultural activity, 14.9% wanted a concert. This measures unmet desire during a year, across events. It cannot set the probability of attending one concert. [INE results](https://www.ine.gob.cl/sala-de-prensa/prensa/general/noticia/2025/08/18/un-75-4-de-las-personas-particip%C3%B3-en-actividades-culturales-al-menos-una-vez-durante-los-%C3%BAltimos-doce-meses)
- EOD Santiago 2012 can describe trip purpose, mode, duration and geography for broad recreation trips by day type. ENUT 2023 can inform the distribution of discretionary time. Use the former to check typical travel times and the latter to check feasible time budgets. Neither gives event-specific interest or a current event's audience. [SECTRA EOD](https://www.sectra.gob.cl/info-territorial/region-metropolitana-de-santiago/) · [INE ENUT](https://www.ine.gob.cl/docs/default-source/uso-del-tiempo-tiempo-libre/metodologias/ii-enut/informe-calidad-ii-enut-2023.pdf)

## First comparison

1. Offer three choices: this event, one specified alternative activity, or stay home. Keep the same synthetic person and the same preference draw in both venues. Use the person's real schedule constraints in the model and an explicit cash allowance. Remove options without a route, enough time, enough cash, or an available place.
2. For each feasible activity, start with an interest score by activity type. Subtract a time penalty and a money penalty for the whole outing. Use posted prices and a route for the day and hour. A normal draw on the utility difference gives a binary probit for attend versus stay. Logit gives a small first model with several alternatives. Keep both the interest scale and cost coefficients as sensitivity assumptions until matched choices support a fit.
3. Freeze the two locations and the expected response before running them. Check whether the same person switches venue, chooses the alternative, or stays when route time or price changes. Preserve fixed jobs and activity preferences across cases. Compare aggregate duration bins with ENPCCL or EOD for the same activity and population.
4. To predict a specific event's origin shares or attendance, validate with a withheld event with measured attendance and observed origin or mode samples. For a fixed audience with supplied shares, check that estimated and unknown origins sum to attendance. Keep that conditional allocation separate from predicting how many people decide to attend.

No event-level Santiago fit, event score or implementation is part of this brief.
