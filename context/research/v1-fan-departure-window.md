## Proposed itinerary timing

- For the replay, assume arrival at the exterior gate between 14:00 inclusive and 15:00 exclusive at both sites. This assumes an hour of pre-match arrivals. The match sources provide no distribution of individual departures.
- For each group with a route, subtract its full door-to-gate route duration from both arrival bounds. Include access walks, waits, travel, transfers, parking or alighting as applicable, and the final walk. For a 45-minute route, the estimated departure window is 13:15–14:15. Publish local clock minutes since midnight.
- Record the service calendar used by the route. The match was on a Sunday. A route built from weekday service is a declared replay assumption. If the route uses a fixed duration from a representative departure, the interval estimates timing for the group.
- Keep people without a route in the attendance total. Report the route coverage and give the unrouted group no departure estimate. Use the same timing rule for both sites so the estimated departures can shift with travel time.
